Support

Practical help for playing, social tables, voice, and purchases.

Play basics · 基本玩法

Play a legal card combination that beats the current table play, or Pass when permitted. Partners sit opposite each other. Follow the in-game turn indicator and result screen for authoritative status.

按当前牌型出更大的合法组合；允许时也可选择“不出”。队友相对而坐，请以牌桌上的轮次提示和结算结果为准。

Campus · 校友房

Choose your university in Campus Lobby, enter its Hall, then choose a table or enter a six-digit Room Number. Campus Social starts with at least two real people; remaining seats may be filled by AI.

在校友房选择大学并进入大厅，可选桌或输入六位房间号。校友社交牌局至少需要两位真人。

Friend Rooms · 好友房

Create a Friend Room and share its six-digit Room Number, or join with a number from a friend. Scheduled rooms open their live-table join window shortly before the scheduled time.

创建好友房后分享六位房间号，也可以输入好友提供的房间号加入。预约牌局会在预定时间前开放入桌。

Friend Voice · 好友语音

Friend Voice requires microphone permission and an active room voice entitlement. Check mute status and network access if others cannot hear you. Voice is live only; Guandan does not configure conversation recording.

好友语音需要麦克风权限及有效的房间语音权益。听不到声音时，请检查静音状态和网络连接。

Hands-Free · 免手操作

Enable Hands-Free during play, grant microphone and speech-recognition permissions, then begin a command with the wake phrase “校友掼蛋”. Speak only on your turn and confirm any ambiguous card choice on screen.

牌局中开启免手操作并允许麦克风、语音识别权限；以唤醒词“校友掼蛋”开始指令。请在自己的回合说出指令。

AI Practice · AI陪练

Open AI Practice from Home or Campus navigation. Training and coaching access depends on the selected product and your current free-use or purchase entitlement. AI Practice is separate from online social matches.

可从首页或校友房导航进入 AI 陪练。训练与教练功能会依据对应产品的免费次数或购买权益开放。

Purchases and Restore · 购买与恢复

Purchases are made through Apple. If access is missing after reinstalling or changing devices, open the relevant purchase screen and choose Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple account. Do not purchase again while an earlier transaction is still processing.

购买由 Apple 处理。重装或更换设备后若权益缺失，请在对应购买页面选择“恢复购买”，并使用同一 Apple 账号。

Account and data deletion · 账号与数据删除

The current app does not have an automated in-app deletion control. Email us with the subject “Guandan data deletion request.” Include your nickname, device type, and approximate last-use date so we can explain how to identify the anonymous account and what data can be removed. Deleting the app clears local app data but does not by itself delete server records.

当前应用内尚无自动注销入口。请以“校友掼蛋数据删除申请”为主题联系我们，并提供昵称、设备类型和大致最后使用日期。卸载应用会清除本机数据，但不会自动删除服务器记录。

Need more help? · 仍需帮助？

Email support@imokfamily.com. Please include your device model, iOS version, app version, the screen involved, and a short description. Do not send payment-card details, passwords, or private voice recordings.
