Privacy Policy

Effective September 26, 2026

1. Scope

This policy describes how Guandan by IMOK (“Guandan”, “we”, or “us”) handles information when you play, use Campus and Friend Room features, use voice features, or make an in-app purchase. Guandan does not sell personal information and does not use app data for cross-app advertising or tracking.

2. Account and profile data

Guandan creates a generated anonymous Firebase account identifier when the app first needs online services. You are not required to provide an email address, phone number, legal name, or social sign-in. The identifier is used to authenticate requests and link your online game, profile, and purchase access.

You may optionally provide a nickname, choose a built-in avatar, and select schools and a city for your profile. These details can be visible to other players in social and game surfaces. The current app does not upload a photo for a custom avatar. The “Add University” form is currently saved only on your device and is not submitted to our servers.

3. Game, room, and social data

To operate online play, we process room and table membership, six-digit room numbers, scheduled-room details and RSVPs, presence and reconnect state, authoritative match state, card actions, outcomes, and game results. Private hands are processed by the authoritative game service and delivered only to the applicable player. Quick-chat messages, reactions, and other conversation messages are stored with the conversation and sender account identifier.

Recent Matches is maintained as an on-device index of authoritative completed matches. The authority also retains match and result records needed for game integrity, reconnect, dispute handling, and commerce eligibility.

4. Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We receive and retain the product identifier, StoreKit transaction identifier and verification payload, purchase environment and status, purchaser account binding, entitlement or room binding, purchase time, and refund or revocation status. An app-account token derived from the anonymous account may be used to bind a transaction to the correct Guandan account. We do not receive your full payment-card or bank-account details from Apple.

5. Friend Room Voice

If you choose Friend Voice and grant microphone permission, microphone audio is transmitted in real time through LiveKit/WebRTC infrastructure to participants in that Friend Room. Guandan’s current implementation does not configure recording or egress and does not store conversational audio. LiveKit receives a short-lived room token and a participant identity derived from your anonymous account so it can connect the correct room participant.

6. Hands-Free speech commands

Hands-Free is optional and starts only when enabled by you. Your device’s operating-system speech-recognition service receives microphone audio and returns recognized words to Guandan. For an online match, Guandan sends the recognized command text—not raw microphone audio—to the authoritative service to validate and execute the command. The current authority processes that text immediately and does not persist the transcript in match storage. Platform speech services may process audio under their own terms and privacy policies.

7. Campus media and local storage

The app may download approved Campus images and entry videos on demand. Local media caches are integrity checked, size limited, and automatically evicted. App preferences, profile copies, purchase-recovery context, Add University drafts, and the Recent Matches index may remain on your device until cleared by the app’s normal cache behavior, app data is reset, or the app is removed.

8. Device, service, and diagnostic information

Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Installations, and Messaging components may process an app installation identifier and limited device/app metadata such as device model, operating-system and app versions, language, time zone, and SDK/network diagnostics. Guandan does not use these identifiers for advertising. The current app does not include Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or Firebase Performance Monitoring.

Our hosting and authority infrastructure creates operational request logs containing items such as request time, route, response status, network address, and service diagnostics. Guandan application code does not intentionally log request bodies or Hands-Free transcripts. Current default Google Cloud operational logs are retained for 30 days.

9. Why we use data

  • Provide authentication, profiles, Campus presence, rooms, matching, gameplay, reconnect, chat, and match history.
  • Verify purchases, restore access, prevent duplicate redemption, and handle refunds or revocations.
  • Provide real-time Friend Voice and optional Hands-Free commands.
  • Protect game integrity, secure the service, diagnose failures, and meet legal obligations.

10. Service providers

We use Apple for App Store purchases and device speech recognition; Google Firebase and Google Cloud for anonymous authentication, data storage, app installation/messaging infrastructure, and authoritative hosting; and LiveKit/WebRTC infrastructure for real-time Friend Voice. These providers process data on our behalf or as platform providers under their own terms.

11. Retention

Profile, room, conversation, match, and entitlement records currently have no automatic user-level expiry and are retained while needed to operate and protect the service. Verified transaction and revocation ledgers may be retained for fraud prevention, accounting, dispute resolution, and legal compliance. Short-lived room codes, presence leases, and voice access tokens expire according to their feature lifecycle. Operational logs currently use the 30-day default described above.

12. Account and data deletion

Current limitation: Guandan does not yet provide an in-app account-deletion control or an automated server-side deletion endpoint.

You may contact support@imokfamily.com to request assistance with access, correction, or deletion. We will explain the information needed to locate an anonymous account and what can be removed. Some verified purchase, security, fraud-prevention, or legal records may need to be retained. Removing the app clears app-managed local data from that device but does not by itself delete server records.

13. Security

We use authenticated requests, encrypted network transport, access controls, server-side purchase verification, and authoritative game-state boundaries designed to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

14. Children

Guandan is not directed to children. The app does not currently perform age verification. If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided information, contact us so we can review the request.

15. International processing

Our service providers and infrastructure may process information in countries or regions other than where you live, subject to applicable safeguards and law.

16. Contact and updates

For privacy questions, contact support@imokfamily.com. We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change and will post the effective date here.
